# FreshRoute v2 — Interface design

## Audience and visual language

Designed for dispatch planners and hackathon judges reviewing a decision, not for a consumer marketplace. The light warm-neutral canvas, white work surfaces, evergreen actions, and restrained sage accents keep the control tower calm. Amber signals attention; red signals a critical constraint or adverse outcome. Text always accompanies color. System fonts avoid network dependencies.

## Structure

Five work areas: Dispatch overview, Inventory & demand, Scenario lab, Financial estimate, and Method & assumptions. The overview puts outcome metrics and a short dispatch brief first, then At-risk batches, route network, comparison, dispatch allocations, compact markdown recommendations, and constraint evidence. The route network is schematic, not geographic.

At-risk rows use consistent produce markers, a short reason, quantity and expiry context, and a simple action tag. They remain compact and link to existing allocation evidence rather than duplicating the planning flow.

## Interaction and accessibility

Inputs show units and assumptions. Scenario changes mark results stale. Scenario comparison shows changes against Current conditions. The freshness timeline is the interaction for allocation evidence. Visible focus, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, responsive stacking, and horizontally scrollable exact-value tables are expected.

Avoid excessive cards, decorative charts, glowing gradients, glass effects, and AI-themed imagery. Keep visuals connected to planning decisions.
